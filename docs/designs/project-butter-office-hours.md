# Design: Project BUTTER — offline-first free iOS music player

## Problem Statement

Free iOS music players are crippled (ads, missing FLAC, poor background playback) and
good players are paid. The audiophile with 500+ GB of FLAC/ALAC on a PC or NAS has no
free-forever player that is also good. Moving music from PC to iPhone is the most
painful step of the switching moment. BUTTER is a free-forever, offline-first iOS music
player built from real Reddit/App Store complaints: no ads, no accounts, no tracking,
no IAP.

## Demand Evidence

- No direct user evidence yet — founder honestly stated: "No evidence yet — Reddit
  complaints are the only signal." The complaints are a proxy, not demand.
- Proxy evidence (8 documented complaints from Reddit/App Store 1-star reviews):
  1. Ads every few songs, crippled free tiers
  2. Background playback flaky in free apps
  3. VLC works but UI is dated; pretty players are paid
  4. FLAC/lossless missing in free players
  5. Apps demand accounts, harvest data
  6. Moving music from PC to iPhone is painful
  7. Lock-screen controls missing artwork/seek
  8. Apps lag with big libraries
- A Reddit thread titled "Why is there no good local audio player for iOS" — the gap
  is real and recurring.
- Flacbox (4.6★, 8.6K ratings) monetizes with ads + $4.99/mo or $19.99/yr or $59.99
  lifetime; its real reviews complain about ads and pop-ups pushing premium, no
  equalizer in free version, library erases if inactive for a day and takes 15 min to
  re-sync, segue/gapless loading takes seconds, crashes on iOS 27.2 beta.
- Doppler ($9 one-time) has real complaints: crashes on lock screen, heavy lag when
  navigating, no free version (paid only after trial), v3 upgrade priced separately.
- Validation plan: Gate 2 ships with the Q5 observation action (watch 1 real person's
  transfer-and-play workflow) before external users.

## Status Quo

The FLAC audiophile pays for Poweramp (Android) or Flacbox/Doppler (iOS) today. Their
complaint is not "lacks an engine" — the paid apps are functional but clunky, and the
free ones are ad-supported. The switching moment is: get music from PC to phone, then
play it. Moving music from PC to iPhone is painful (complaint #6) — that's where the
status quo breaks down. Doppler is $9 one-time and beautiful; the audiophile already
paid for it or Flacbox. The incumbent is the paid app.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- **The person:** The FLAC audiophile — has 500+ GB of FLAC/ALAC on a PC or NAS, owns
  decent headphones or a DAC, pays for Poweramp/Flacbox today, and their complaint is
  the paid apps are clunky.
- **The wedge:** Free-forever + better than the paid apps. The narrowest version worth
  shipping: Gates 0-2 first (audio engine + import + Wi-Fi transfer + basic library),
  no pretty UI. Ship to the audiophile, then build the UI (Gate 3) with their feedback.
- **Not the user:** the ad-complainer who won't pay for anything and won't sideload —
  they're the crowd, not the wedge.

## Constraints

- Constitution (CLAUDE.md §0): No ads, no ad SDKs, no accounts, no tracking, no IAP,
  free forever, MIT license, no GPL code copying (namida/harmonoid are study-only),
  no streaming-from-YouTube, no piracy-adjacent features. Content = user's own files
  only.
- Windows constraint: zero local iOS tooling; all macOS steps live in CI (GitHub
  Actions macOS runners). Sideloadly for device installs.
- Performance is a feature: 120fps ProMotion-capable UI, cold start < 1.5s, zero
  visible jank on library scroll — checked at every gate.
- Distribution: Sideloadly (7-day resigning) through Gates 0-4 — an explicit, sanctioned
  amendment to CLAUDE.md's "via TestFlight" gate-checklist wording; TestFlight only at
  Gate 5 ($99 Developer Program enrollment deferred to Gate 5).

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Gate-ordered as planned
Follow CLAUDE.md's gate structure as written: Gate 1 audio engine (just_audio +
audio_service, background playback, lock screen, interruptions) → Gate 2
library/import (file_picker, Wi-Fi transfer, metadata, 1k-song stress test). One
explicit, sanctioned amendment: the distribution path (see Distribution Plan) uses
Sideloadly in place of TestFlight for Gates 0-4 — CLAUDE.md Gate 0's "via TestFlight"
checklist wording, Gate 1's "TestFlight build #" evidence wording, and §5 Definition of
Done rule 2's "verified on the real iPhone via TestFlight build" wording are all
superseded by this design's Sideloadly plan, sanctioned by the founder (premise 5
defense). For Gates 0-4, "verified on the real iPhone via TestFlight build" reads
"verified on the real iPhone via Sideloadly-installed build"; the TestFlight wording
reverts to literal at Gate 5.
**Pros:** audio engine is the #1 Reddit complaint category; zero deviation from the
gate structure; Q5 observation action already logged as a standing item mitigates the
wedge risk. **Cons:** the switching moment (transfer) is delayed to Gate 2; the
distribution risk is carried through Gates 0-2. **Effort:** M/L per gate. **Risk:**
Medium (distribution risk accepted and documented).
